### October 2, 2026

Today I completed a professional photoshoot of the BEHOLDER capture rig with photographer Mackenzie Katsmar. We spent several hours in the studio taking clean, professional photographs of the completed system for use in the final report and project documentation.

I also continued work on the photogrammetry processing pipeline. I configured an older third computer to process smaller COLMAP/OpenMVS datasets and made further changes to keep the automation portable between different machines. Processing of the Scenic Caves dataset continued, with the reconstruction reaching the later mesh and texturing stages. I also investigated cloud GPU rental through Lambda as a possible option for processing the very large ~17,000-image dataset.

Further work was completed on the Three.js viewer, including object selection, transform controls for moving, rotating and scaling models, and undo/redo support.
